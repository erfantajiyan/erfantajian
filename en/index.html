My Story

This is where my journey is told — from the first robot I built to the things I'm building with AI today.

01 2015 – 2016

The kid who built robots

I've loved robotics since childhood. In 2015 and 2016, I competed in the Arad robotics competition in the soccer robot category and placed 5th and 4th nationally.

That's where I realized how much I love building something with my own hands that moves.

02 2017 – 2019

When I realized code alone wasn't enough

In 2017, I failed the middle school entrance exam. But that same year, my friend Ali introduced me to Unity.

That's when I realized that just writing code wasn't enough for me — I love art too.

Photoshop Illustrator Premiere FL Studio 3ds Max Unity
But my favorite part was Level Design — the place where code and art meet.

03 2020 – 2021

A hard but sweet year

I was in ninth grade and the stress of the high school entrance exam was intense — but I made it and got into SAMPAD.

That same year, I interned at the game studio Fani and learned After Effects and Blender.

The next year, I entered the world of Full-Stack.

HTML CSS JavaScript Python Django

Of course, I couldn't fully continue down the backend path, because school took up a big part of my time.

Starting to learn Full-Stack
04 2022 – 2023

The start of a team, the start of a new path

In late 2021, I met Hesam at a Game Show. The two of us formed the team "Shahr-e Soukhte" and took first place right from the start.

This team later grew into Asuri.

During these years, Ali, Hesam, and I joined various startups and earned top ranks in several competitions.

This is where I learned that building with a team is 10 times more enjoyable than building alone.
Team Asuri building together
First place at Game Show
05 2023 – 2025

The year everything got hard

2022 was the hardest year of my life. I don't want to go into the details of that period, but for a while I distanced myself from the world I had built.

Still, I didn't give up. I focused on the 2024 university entrance exam and finally got into Abadan University of Petroleum Industry.

Even when everything is dark, you can still get back up.
2022 Hard time ↓ 2024 Comeback ↓ 2024 Abadan University of Petroleum Industry
06 2026 – 2027

Returning to my roots with AI

In 2025, we started building bigger games with team Asuri — the team had grown and we worked on the SCP project.

But the biggest change for me happened in 2026. I decided to combine everything I had learned over the years with AI.

Magic Note Pad

A note-taking app with semantic search that I built with the help of AI.

Prompt Search Engine

A search engine for finding the best prompts.

Here I realized once again that everything I've learned over the years can come together.
